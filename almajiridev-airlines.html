About AlmajiriDev Airlines

AlmajiriDev Airlines is the only airline we know of that was shipped from a terminal (the code kind, not the airport kind). Proudly rooted in Nigeria, we connect the country’s major cities with flights that are safe, reliable and — unlike our git history — never force-pushed. From our hub in Enugu, we link travellers to Lagos, Abuja, Kano and beyond, bringing business, family and culture closer together.

We run on a strict zero-downtime policy, which is impressive because our pilots still can’t get sudo on the cockpit. Our cabins have no merge conflicts, our boarding groups are sorted in O(n log n), and our delays are the only bugs we document honestly — they’re features, we checked with QA. Book with us and remember: on AlmajiriDev, every landing is a successful deployment.
